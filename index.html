Ride ideas

    Good to know

    • Bikes travel free on Melbourne's metro trains. They are not allowed on trams, and generally not on buses, including rail replacement buses, so check for planned works on your lines before you go at transport.vic.gov.au.
    • Ride times assume a relaxed 15 km/h. Train times are typical all-stops times from the timetable, plus an allowance for each change.
    • A few rides use short stretches of road to get between a station and a trail. These are shown dashed on the map.
